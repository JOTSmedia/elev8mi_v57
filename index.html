    ELEV8MI is a tapestry. The words “elevate me” name something simple: each of us can be lifted — physically, emotionally, spiritually — by another person. When a higher vibration is felt, that change becomes internal, and the energy can be shared with intent. It keeps going, always evolving. Purple is the flow that opens when intuition guides how we express ourselves. Together, ELEV8MI gives your wings the power of flight, so you can heal and elevate yourself, and then the people around you.

    A little guidance before you draw

    The face-down cards are waiting for you. Choose a reading style from the dropdown to light up the shuffled deck: general guidance, relationships, career, personal growth, or another focus.

    One card gives you a single theme. Three cards offer three perspectives: past, present, and what may unfold. You can change your style and spread, or shuffle again, before your first pick.

    Choose your cards from the lit deck. Each selected card appears in your reading. When your spread is complete, you can read the meanings and open an email draft for Allison’s interpretation.

    A reversed card is another angle on the theme, not a bad omen. Use the reading as a prompt for reflection, rather than a guaranteed prediction.
